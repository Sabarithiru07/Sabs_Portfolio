Smoking Detection System

Detects smoking in restricted zones and triggers instant alerts.

Computer VisionDjango

Overview

A computer-vision system that detects smoking in restricted zones and triggers instant alert notifications, helping keep offices, schools, hospitals and public spaces smoke-free.

The problem

Smoking rules in restricted areas are hard to enforce with manual patrols alone, and the fire and health risks are real.

Key features

AI-powered detection of smoking behaviour in video feeds

Instant alerts to security or authorities

Continuous real-time monitoring

Incident reports and analytics to track compliance

How it works

  1. 1

    Camera feeds from restricted zones are monitored.

  2. 2

    Computer vision detects smoking behaviour in the footage.

  3. 3

    An alert notification is triggered immediately.

  4. 4

    The incident is recorded so patterns can be reviewed and compliance improved.
